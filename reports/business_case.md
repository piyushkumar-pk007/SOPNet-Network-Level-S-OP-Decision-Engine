# Business Case

Large supply chains need to balance demand, production, transportation, inventory, and service levels. Traditional planning often works in silos. This project demonstrates how forecasting, optimization, and simulation can be combined into one decision engine for S&OP.

## Questions the project answers
- What will demand look like over the next four weeks?
- Which plant should produce which category and how much?
- Which DC should carry inventory?
- How should product be allocated to retail nodes?
- What is the service-level risk once uncertainty is introduced?
- What changes when demand, cost, or capacity assumptions shift?

## Why public data plus synthetic network
The M5 dataset is strong for demand analytics but does not include a real plant and distribution network. To make the planning problem end-to-end, the project generates a realistic synthetic supply network and links it to the M5 demand data.
